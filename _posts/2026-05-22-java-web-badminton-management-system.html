Badminton System

A Java Spring Boot–based badminton management system. It integrates user management, court reservations, match organization, appraisal/review, and online payment. Designed for badminton venues, training centers, and clubs, it helps administrators and players manage activities efficiently.

User Management – Register/login, profile management. Court Reservation – Book badminton courts; includes custom queries. Match Management – Schedule and manage tournaments. Appraisal/Review – Allow users to post and view reviews of venues or services. Payment Integration – Includes Alipay interface for online transactions. Administration – Modules for administrators to handle users, reservations, and operations. Statistics & Reports – ShowStatisticalResults controller suggests data analysis and visualization of usage.

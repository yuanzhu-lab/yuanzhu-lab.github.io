A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the sixteen identities in my 2022 tutorial PolyLog Integrals, completed in Oct 2026.

LeanPolyLog

What it proves

Sixteen log-trigonometric and polylogarithmic integrals, with closed forms in π, log 2, Catalan’s constant G, ζ(3), Li4(½) and Im Li3((1+i)/2). For example:

  • Li2(i) = −π²/48 + iG
  • Li2((√5−1)/2) = π²/10 − log²φ
  • ∫01 log(1+x²)/x dx = π²/24
  • ∫0π/2 x log² sin x dx
  • ∫01 Li3(x)/(x√(1−x²)) dx

All sixteen are proven, with no sorry, and every proof depends only on Lean’s standard axioms.

How it is checked

  • The Lean kernel checks every proof.
  • A CI test fails if any proof picks up sorry or a non-standard axiom.
  • Lean cannot check that a statement means what was intended, so each statement also has a numerical twin evaluated with mpmath at 80 digits; CI requires agreement to 60 digits.

Notes

  • Mathlib has no polylogarithms or Catalan’s constant yet, so Li s z, G and ζ(3) are defined by their series.
  • Mathlib has no half-disc Cauchy theorem, so one identity is proven by differentiating in the radius instead of integrating over a contour.
  • Writing it down formally caught one error in the 2022 tutorial (a corrected value of π²/24).
  • The Lean code was written with AI assistance (Claude); every statement and proof was reviewed by me.

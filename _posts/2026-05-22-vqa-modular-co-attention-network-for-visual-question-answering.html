My undergraduate innovation project, completed in Mar 2024.

MCAN-VQA

The work is based on the well-known MCAN-VQA model (Modular Co-Attention Networks for Visual Question Answering, CVPR 2019). The original implementation was in PyTorch, and my contribution was to lead a team of three members in migrating and adapting the model to the MindSpore framework.

Framework Migration – Converted the PyTorch-based MCAN-VQA into MindSpore, ensuring functional equivalence. Code Refactoring – Adjusted data loading, training loops, and optimizer definitions to align with MindSpore APIs. Hyperparameter Tuning – Fine-tuned learning rate, batch size, optimizer settings after migration. Training & Validation – Successfully trained the MindSpore version to reach the expected benchmark results, matching the original PyTorch baseline. Documentation – Provided clear setup and usage instructions for MindSpore users.

A benchmark for evaluating how creative large language models are at designing magic tricks, completed in Dec 2024.

Creative Magic Benchmark

Idea

Each magic trick in the dataset has a setting (location, props, optionally an image) and one or more reference schemes. A model proposes new schemes, which are judged from three viewpoints:

  • Setter: does the design use the given props and location?
  • Audience: is the effect entertaining to watch?
  • Performer: is it actually performable?

Scoring

Each viewpoint multiplies a convergence term (an LLM judge checks the constraint is met) by a divergence term (the fraction of extracted designs, effects or techniques that are not already in a list of common ones). The total is a weighted sum:

Score = α · ScoreS + β · ScoreA + γ · ScoreP

Details

  • Whether an idea counts as “common” is decided by spaCy similarity against the predefined lists.
  • If a trick comes with an image, a ViLT model extracts its objects and adds them to the props.
  • LLM judgments are averaged over several calls to reduce instability.
